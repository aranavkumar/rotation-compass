Methodology & sources

What the evidence represents

Measured information
Farm Setup records the stated basis, source and date of a soil texture observation. A measurement is user-supplied. Sample farm values are illustrative.
Model estimates
NASA POWER supplies gridded meteorology for the district. Seasonal totals and means summarize those estimates. Crop occupation dates use reference durations. Scenario adjustments are hypothetical stress tests.
Derived indicators
Climate and water points summarize historical comparisons. Diversity counts distinct crops and family changes. The weighted overall score expresses your priorities on a 0–100 scale.
Qualitative assessments
Drainage, irrigation access, salinity and planned residue retention are field assumptions. Feasibility describes how a rotation meets the configured pilot checks.

Review and limitations

Team Dracarys used OpenAI Codex for software, synthetic examples, checks and documentation. The ranking engine applies explicit rules. NASA POWER supplied the historical record.

Phase 6 checked software behavior against the configured Punjab reference systems. Independent agricultural review and farmer usability testing have yet to take place.
