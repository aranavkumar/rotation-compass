Rotation Explorer

Explain the shortlist

The engine checks all 27 rotations. At the sample priorities, maize → wheat → rice scores 64.9/100. The current practice scores 61.5.

Crop diversity improves. Climate suitability drops. Unresolved cultivar schedules leave other options, including chickpea rotations, unranked.

Scenario Explorer · Hypothetical stress test

A change in priorities can change the first choice

Reduce seasonal rain by 50%, add 3 °C and raise climate priority to 100. Keep the other three weights at 25.

Maize → wheat → maize moves from third to first among scoreable alternatives. The historical record stays available alongside the scenario.

Scenario Explorer · Irrigation access

What if irrigation is unavailable?

The reference rice and wheat systems require irrigation. Turning it off marks the current practice newly infeasible. Reset restores the baseline.

Seasonal rainfall alone cannot tell this field how much water to apply.
